# Artomango brand files

- `mango.svg` (also `/favicon.svg`, and every icon and share image made from it):
  the mango from **Noto Emoji** by Google, https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji
  Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
  Used unchanged as the Artomango mark.
- `og-image.jpg`: the link preview shown when someone shares an Artomango page.

Brand rules (short):
- The mark is always this mango drawing, never a typed emoji, so it looks the same everywhere.
- Next to the name: mark, then "Artomango" in Darker Grotesque 800, ink #111110.
- Backgrounds: paper #FBF8F0 or white. The mango needs no tile, outline or shadow.
